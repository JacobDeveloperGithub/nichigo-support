Privacy Policy

Information NichiGo stores

NichiGo does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. Study progress, preferences, and quiz history are stored locally on your device. The developer does not operate an account or analytics server and does not receive this local study data.

Widgets

Current-word and schedule information is shared locally with NichiGo’s iOS widget extension through the app’s private App Group container. This information is used only to display and update NichiGo widgets on your device.

Notifications

New-word notifications are optional and are scheduled on your device after you grant permission. You can disable them in NichiGo or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.

Optional tips

Optional tips are consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. Tips do not unlock features. NichiGo does not receive or store your payment-card information. Apple processes purchases under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

External links

Links to source repositories, licenses, Apple, or GitHub open outside NichiGo. Those services may handle information according to their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Your study information remains on your device until you reset progress or delete the app. Reset Progress clears local learning history. Deleting NichiGo removes its remaining local app data. Because NichiGo has no user accounts or remote user profiles, there is no server-side profile to retrieve or delete.

Changes to this policy

If NichiGo’s data practices change, this page and the in-app notice will be updated with a new effective date.

Vocabulary Sources & Licenses

The Japanese Language Proficiency Test does not publish official vocabulary lists. NichiGo’s level assignments are community estimates based on human-created vocabulary lists, and NichiGo is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Japan Foundation, Japan Educational Exchanges and Services, or the JLPT.

Source entries and definitions were retained without substantive rewriting. Local processing normalized formatting, capitalization, and punctuation; removed duplicate cards; and generated accepted-answer variants required for quiz functionality.
